# Creepy Levitation

Creepy Levitation is a 3D art project I created in September 2024, exploring a darker and more unsettling visual direction.

## Background

With this project, I moved beyond standard design to experiment with a darker, more visceral art style. The work combines 3D modeling, atmospheric lighting, classical architectural elements, and surreal compositions to create a horror-inspired visual world.

## Core Idea

The project explores the feeling of intrusive thoughts and internal experiences through a surreal physical environment. The levitating figure and surrounding elements are designed to create a quiet but unsettling atmosphere.

The main goal was to create a distinct emotional response from the viewer while maintaining a visually cohesive and mysterious scene.

## Process

I created the 3D environment and arranged the different elements to build the final composition. I worked with volumetric lighting, suspended physics, materials, and camera composition to establish the atmosphere of the piece.
